In October 2026 I joined [SigNoz](https://github.com/SigNoz/signoz), an open source observability platform built on OpenTelemetry. I work on alerting, in the Go backend and the React frontend.

A good alert tells you something you need to act on. A bad one wakes you at 3am for nothing, and after a few of those you stop trusting any of them. So far I've added a configuration area for notification channels, routing policies and planned downtime, plus search and saved views for the alert rules list.
